# YouTube Content Packaging

## Overview

Package ideas so they earn the click (title + thumbnail) and hold attention (hook + story cycles). Integrates Aprilynne Alter's full workflow with Jake Thomas click triggers, Brandon Sanderson-style Promise-Progress-Payoff, and cross-validated best practices on evaluation, faces/contrast, and retention.

**Standing rules (aligned with curiosity-driven-scripture-journey):**
- Output to the user stays lean. Content written for the public reader is expanded, interesting, and conversational (still never using “we/us/our” as if sitting beside the reader).
- Descriptions and pre-click copy must leave an additional open curiosity gap and support dual-audience design (early CTA + continued tension). Vary structure deliberately.
- Every generated file identifies the creating agent.

## Instructions

Work in sequence. Never skip packaging for "just good content."

### 1. Idea Evaluation (after generation)
Filter on two primary axes plus secondary checks:
- **Reach / TAM**: Estimate upper-bound views by studying similar videos. Prefer ideas with proven outliers above your channel average.
- **Remarkability / Novelty**: Does the combination feel fresh? Humans seek novelty.
- Feasibility with current resources.
- Brand / mission alignment.
- Secondary goals (leads, relationships, experimentation) may intentionally lower TAM.

Reject or defer ideas that fail both reach and novelty unless strategic reasons exist.

### 2. Click Triggers
Confirm the packaging will hit Desire, Fear, or Curiosity (ideally two). Titles and thumbnails must open a gap the video closes honestly.

### 3. Title + Thumbnail Pair
- Generate volume of titles (see title-writing skill).
- Design thumbnail that visualizes the gap (see thumbnail-design skill).
- Ensure the pair is skimable in 2 seconds and promises what the video delivers.

### 4. Video Hook (first 5–30 seconds)
- Immediately confirm the plot promise and format promise of the packaging.
- Front-load value or stimulus.
- Keep intro under ~25–40 seconds.
- Establish USP early (expertise, effort, proof, unique access).

### 5. Story / Value Cycles (Promise → Progress → Payoff)
Structure the body as repeating micro-cycles:
- **Promise**: Open a small curiosity or value gap.
- **Progress**: Deliver insight, framework, or story movement.
- **Payoff**: Close the gap and open the next one.
Applies to educational content (thesis → support → conclusion) and narrative (character + obstacle + resolution). Use emotional stakes even in how-to videos. Edit ruthlessly; expand/contract time for importance. Exaggerate for clarity without fabricating facts.

### 6. Content System Habits
- Daily idea generation.
- Separate generation from filtration.
- Study model channels and outliers regularly.
- Test packaging iteratively (title/thumbnail swaps).
- Prefer authentic emotional delivery over pure polish in saturated environments.

### Output for a Full Session
For any given idea deliver:
- Evaluation score (reach + remarkability + feasibility/alignment)
- Top 3 titles with triggers noted
- Thumbnail description or direction
- Hook script outline (first 15–30 s)
- High-level story-cycle map for the body
- One suggested series angle if applicable

Always prioritize honesty: packaging that over-promises destroys long-term distribution.
