|---------|--------|
| prophets | profits (when meaning biblical prophets) |
| omniscient | omnisient |

| reign | rain (when kings/rule) |
| judicial | Judicial mid-sentence |

## Quotation policy
- Inside **KJV (or other version) quotes**: preserve the version’s spelling/wording (`brake`, `thou`, `sanctuary shall be cleansed`).
- Outside quotes: modern standard spelling and the forms in this table.
- EGW book titles: standard estate titles — *The Great Controversy*, *Prophets and Kings*, *The Desire of Ages*, *Testimonies*, etc.
