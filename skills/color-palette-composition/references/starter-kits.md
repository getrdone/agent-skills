## Study patterns from established practice

Use patterns to learn quantity and hierarchy, never names or specifications to clone.

| Pattern | What to learn |
|---|---|
| Deep ink/navy + warm off-white + brass | A dark jewel-like partner gains warmth from a small metallic spark; obtain manufacturer sample for actual paint. |
| Greige + related stone + deep green | Undertone harmony makes a dark accent feel intentional rather than cold. |
| IBM Carbon-like surfaces + one interactive blue | Near-monochrome UI surfaces leave one action color unmistakable. |
| Material-like semantic roles | `surface` and `on-surface` do most reading work; accent need not flood the screen. |
| Swiss poster: white + black + one red | Extreme value structure plus one chroma can be more forceful than a colorful palette. |
| Teal + orange cinema | A cool environment plus scarce warm practical/subject light creates immediate focus. |
