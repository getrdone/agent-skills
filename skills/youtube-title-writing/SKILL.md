# YouTube Title Writing

## Overview

Produce 20–30 title variations per video that create irresistible curiosity gaps while remaining honest. Primary framework from Aprilynne Alter + Jake Thomas click triggers (Desire, Fear, Curiosity), cross-validated against widespread 2025–2026 title formula data (number + benefit + timeframe, result-without-method, transformation, contrarian).

## Instructions

Always generate volume first (20–30 options). Select and refine later. Target under 65 characters; front-load the most important words (left side is visible first).

### Three Click Triggers (Jake Thomas)
Every strong title must evoke one or two of these:
1. **Desire** — Viewer wants the outcome (result, status, skill, money, freedom).
2. **Fear** — Viewer wants to avoid the pain or loss (mistakes, missing out, failure, regret).
3. **Curiosity** — Open loop or novelty (what happened, the missing piece, the unexpected result).

Prefer combinations: Curiosity + Desire or Curiosity + Fear.

### Core Process (under 30 minutes)
1. Brainstorm freely from the idea (5 min).
2. Study 3–5 model channels with similar formats (not necessarily same niche). Extract structural patterns.
3. Review topic outliers (high-performing videos on the subject). Note recurring structures, numbers, timeframes.
4. Feed best candidates + constraints into AI for additional variations. Specify likes, dislikes, and required triggers.
5. Score each against triggers, length, clarity, and honesty.

### Key Formulas
- **RO (Results / Objections / Timeline)**: Combine in any order. Example: "Learn Spanish even if you've never spoken a foreign language before in 9 weeks."
- Result without method: "I Saved $20,000 Last Year on a $45k Salary — Here's the System"
- Number + Benefit + Timeframe: "5 Ways to Double Your YouTube Views in 30 Days"
- Transformation: "How I Went From [specific low] to [specific high] in [time]"
- Contrarian / Hidden detail: "The Mistake Every New Creator Makes" or "What Nobody Tells You About X"
- Extreme commitment: "I Analyzed 1,000 Viral Thumbnails — Here's the Pattern"

### Rules
- 5th-grade reading level or simpler. Prefer short synonyms.
- Create at least one open question in the viewer's mind.
- Optimize primarily for browse/suggested (homepage) traffic; sprinkle high-volume low-competition keywords only when they fit naturally.
- Never promise what the video cannot deliver. Clickbait kills retention and future distribution.
- Keep important words in the first half of the title.

### Output
List 10–20 strong candidates ranked by estimated trigger strength + clarity. Note which triggers each uses. Flag the top 3 for A/B testing or thumbnail pairing.

Hand the chosen title to thumbnail-design skill for visual alignment.
