# YouTube Idea Generation

## Overview

Systematically produce remarkablenovel video ideas by treating every idea as a unique combination of familiar elements. Separate generation from evaluation. Base process on Aprilynne Alter's framework (validated against standard creative ideation and top-creator practices such as daily timed brainstorming used by Ryan Tran and others).

## Instructions

Generate ideas daily as a non-negotiable habit. Set a 10-minute timer and write without judgment.

Treat an idea as Topic + Format + Angle (or more elements). Expand one seed into 10–50 variants by remixing components.

### Core Components
- **Topic**: The subject matter (e.g., Bible prophecy fulfillment, nutrition habits, green card process, gardening).
- **Format**: Delivery vehicle (tutorial, challenge, breakdown, case study, commentary, list, experiment, interview, story, before/after).
- **Angle**: Differentiator that adds novelty or emotional charge (time constraint, money/result, vs comparison, beginner vs expert, contrarian, personal struggle, extreme quantity, specific audience).

### Generation Process
1. Start with a seed topic or recent outlier video.
2. List 5–10 formats that could deliver it.
3. List 5–10 angles that make it remarkable.
4. Systematically combine (topic × format × angle) to produce dozens of candidates.
5. Capture everything raw first. Do not filter for feasibility, brand fit, or market size yet.

### Expansion Techniques
- Take a successful video and change only one component (new format, new angle, new audience).
- Combine two familiar things that have not been paired (e.g., "Daniel 11 prophecy" + "Kitchen Nightmares style diagnosis").
- Use model channels outside your niche for format inspiration while keeping your topic.
- Ask "What would make this 10× more specific / extreme / personal / visual?"

### Output Format
Present ideas as short phrases ready for packaging:
- Topic | Format | Angle
- One-line concept
- Why it might be remarkable (novelty note)

After generation, hand off to evaluation skill or packaging skills. Do not evaluate during this phase.

## Supporting Principles
- Novelty drives clicks more than pure educational value for browse traffic.
- Ideas that feel "already done" still work when the combination is fresh.
- Daily volume compounds; quality emerges from quantity + later filtering.
