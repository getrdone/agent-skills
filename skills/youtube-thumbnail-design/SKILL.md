# YouTube Thumbnail Design

Create glanceable thumbnails that stop the scroll and reinforce the title's curiosity gap. Three C's: Content, Composition, Contrast. Design for mobile first (~200–320 px wide). Communicate in under 2 seconds.

Prioritize faces, result/transform visuals, comparison, novelty. Text 3–5 words max. Primary subject 40–60% of frame. High contrast. Tease, do not reveal. Align with the title gap. First 5 seconds of the video must confirm the promise.
