# YouTube Thumbnail Design

## Overview

Create glanceable thumbnails that stop the scroll and reinforce the title's curiosity gap. Framework drawn from Aprilynne Alter's Three C's (Content, Composition, Contrast) plus data-backed practices from 2025–2026 studies (vidIQ and others): faces in ~70% of breakouts, high contrast, limited text, clear hierarchy.

## Instructions

**Always** load [`skills/dynamic-symmetry/SKILL.md`](../dynamic-symmetry/SKILL.md) before composing. Default crop for YouTube landscape ≈ **root 3** (near 16:9). Stamp rectangle + armature + focal; write Dynamic Symmetry in full in any generation prompt (never “DS”).

Design for mobile feed size first (roughly 200–320 px wide). The thumbnail must communicate in under 2 seconds (glance test).

### Three C's Process
1. **Content** — Decide what visual elements represent the title/idea. Prioritize:
   - Faces (strongest scroll stopper; emotional expression preferred). Use creator face for loyal audience or familiar/recognizable faces for broader reach.
   - Result / transformation visuals (before-after, success state).
   - Comparison (side-by-side, tier list, vs).
   - Novelty or pattern-interrupt object.
   - Minimal supporting text (3–5 words max, bold, high-contrast stroke).
2. **Composition** — Establish hierarchy so the eye lands on the most important element first.
   - Use Dynamic Symmetry (root 3 / phi / etc. + basic armature or MAD) — not rule-of-thirds as the default.
   - Position: primary subject on a strong diagonal/eye; secondary on related echoes.
   - Size: primary element fills 40–60% of frame.
   - Depth/blur: soft background or selective focus to isolate subject.
3. **Contrast** — Make the subject pop against the feed and against competitors.
   - Luminosity: bright subject on dark (or reverse).
   - Color: complementary pairs (orange/teal, yellow/violet, red/cyan).
   - Saturation: high-sat subject against lower-sat background.
   - Avoid low-contrast or monochrome without a saturation pop.

### Proven Formats
- Result: show the desirable outcome.
- Transform: clear before → after.
- Comparison / vs.
- Extreme expression + object.
- Hybrid face (real photo + enhanced background) preferred over fully AI-polished faces in current algorithm climate.

### Practical Rules
- Pass the squint test and the across-the-room test.
- Text is supporting, not primary. Visuals process faster than words.
- Align tightly with the title's curiosity gap; do not repeat the title verbatim.
- Generate multiple variations and A/B test (YouTube Studio or third-party tools).
- Iterate: if CTR is low, change one variable at a time (face expression, background color, text placement).

### Output
Describe or specify:
- Primary subject and expression
- Supporting elements
- Color/contrast strategy
- Text overlay (exact wording + placement)
- Hierarchy rationale
- Why it creates or reinforces the curiosity gap

Pair with the chosen title from the title-writing skill. Test for honesty: the first 5 seconds of the video must confirm the packaging promise.
