# Resources, authority, and plain-language scope

## Core rule (historicist · Reformation · pioneer)

1. **The Bible is the only rule of faith and practice.** Start and end with Scripture. Test every other voice by the Bible.
2. **The Bible is a complete enough revelation for faith.** Do not treat later books as a second Bible.
3. **Bible compared with Bible.** Read each passage in its setting. Clear texts help hard ones.
4. **Historicism:** read prophecy as a long path through history unless the text itself forces another frame.
5. **Reformation and Adventist pioneer light help; they never replace Scripture.**
6. **Ellen White and the pioneers point to Jesus and the Word** — cite carefully; never invent quotes.

## Plain-language core (teaching only — do not over-apply)

**What it is for:** the *teaching* layer — Bible study body copy, Scripture Journey page explanations, evidence steps, definitions, and “rewrite this study for someone new to the Bible.”

**Rules in that layer:** everyday words; spell out “verses 8–11” (never “vv.”); explain hard terms; no empty church jargon (“orthodoxy,” “laity,” etc.) unless defined in the next breath; short clear sentences.

### What plain language must NOT block

| Keep free | Notes |
|-----------|--------|
| **Title / idea matrix** | Full 17×4 creative packaging still required |
| **Hooks, open loops, story craft** | PVSS, retention, narrative voice |
| **Specialized copywriting skillsets** | Any loaded marketing/creative/copy skill stays in force for titles, ads, hooks, CTAs, scripts, and brand voice—within high-trust ethics |
| **Creative writing** | Metaphor, scene, dialogue, pacing, tone variation |
| **Thumbnail / visual concepts** | Not flattened by “simple words only” |
| **Channel voice** | Warm, vivid, or literary delivery OK when the user or brand asks |

**Rule of thumb:**  
- **Explain the Bible simply.**  
- **Package and write creatively** where packaging, titles, hooks, or a specialized copy skill apply.  
- If both appear in one deliverable: simple in the *study answer*; skilled craft in the *package, hook, and call*—without fear, false urgency, or overclaim.

Conflict resolution: high-trust ethics (no manipulation) win over any copy trick. Plain-language teaching never cancels the title matrix or a user-requested creative/copy skill. Honest curiosity gaps in titles, thumbnails, and pre-click copy are not manipulation; empty reaction bait, unsupported insinuation, and delayed teaching answers are. See `youtube-planning.md` → Honest curiosity vs manufactured suspense.
