# Copy-ready checklists & formulas

## Idea combination
`Idea = Topic × Format × Angle`
- **Formats:** tutorial · challenge · commentary/reaction · breakdown/video-essay · vlog · podcast/interview
- **Angles:** beginner · expert · money · versus/comparison · time-bound · contrarian · behind-the-scenes

## Idea filter (both must pass)
- [ ] **TAM** — found outliers (videos beating their channel's own average) on this topic?
- [ ] Outlier view range ≥ my view goal?
- [ ] **Remarkability** — novel enough to be remarked upon? If no prior proof, am I willing to create my own?

## Title generation (30+ in ~30 min)
1. 5 min from your head
2. 5–10 min extract format patterns from your 5 model channels ("how to ___ without ___")
3. 5–10 min outlier tools (1of10) → recurring power words
4. 5 min expansion, seeded with your own list + likes/dislikes → "give me 50 more"

## Title rules
- [ ] < ~65 characters, skimmable
- [ ] Most important words upper-left
- [ ] ~3rd to 5th-grade reading level; short synonyms
- [ ] Exactly one strong curiosity gap
- [ ] Simple language, unless audience is solution-aware & searching a technical term
- [ ] AB test up to 3–5

## RO(T) headline formula (any order, use 1–3)
- **R**esult — "Learn Spanish"
- **O**bjection — "even if you've never spoken a foreign language"
- **T**imeline — "in 9 weeks"

## Click triggers (pick one per title)
- **Desire** — wants an outcome
- **Fear** — wants to avoid a loss
- **Curiosity** — wants to know an outcome
Broad = more reach; narrow = more qualified/conversion.

## Title categories for the Packaging Report
Evaluate **every** category for every video — never skip one (if it's a weak fit, still give 4 or 5 titles and say so). Generate **4 or 5 titles per category**. Keep one strong curiosity gap per title. After listing all categories, mark the single best-fit category, then ASK the user which category/titles to build thumbnails from before doing Phase 2.

### Core (always)
- **🔍 Curiosity** — opens an information gap the viewer must click to close.
- **😱 Fear / Loss** — a risk, mistake, or warning to avoid.
- **✨ Desire / Hope** — a wanted outcome or transformation.
- **🔎 Search-friendly / SEO** — keyword-forward, phrased how people search; use for solution-aware/technical topics.
- **🪞 Relatability / Identity** — "if you're a ___, this is for you."
- **🏆 Authority / Proof** — numbers, named results, credibility, receipts.
- **⚡ Contrarian / Novelty** — myth-bust, "nobody tells you," the surprising angle.

### Strong additional (add when they clearly fit)
- **⏰ Urgency / Timely** — creates pressure with a time element, current event, deadline, or “now vs later.” Use when the topic has a natural clock or recent trigger that raises stakes.
- **❤️ Story / Emotional** — leans on personal narrative, transformation story, or emotional stakes. Use when a human story or felt impact opens a stronger gap than pure information.
- **❓ Question** — poses a direct question the viewer wants answered. Use when a clean question form creates a tighter open loop than a statement title.
- **🛡️ Warning / Protection** — highlights a risk, common trap, deception, or way to stay safe. Use when the core value is avoiding a specific mistake or threat.
- **📜 Pattern / Fulfillment** — shows that something predicted, claimed, or written is now visible or happening. Use for “this was said… and here is the evidence” angles.
- **🌱 Growth / Next Step** — focuses on practical change, improvement, or how to actually live it out. Use when the video ends with an actionable takeaway or identity shift.
- **🔄 Comparison / Contrast** — sets two views, methods, or outcomes against each other. Use when the strongest curiosity comes from “A vs B” or “what most people think vs what works.”

### Situational / secondary (add only if they strengthen packaging)
- **👥 Community / Belonging** — speaks to “people like us” or a shared identity group. Overlaps with Relatability; use when group identity is the primary pull.
- **📖 Source Spotlight** — centers a specific document, quote, data set, primary source, or key text as the curiosity driver.
- **🔥 Challenge / Call** — soft invitation or challenge to act or decide. Use sparingly and keep tone brand-appropriate.

Then: name the **strongest-fit** category (+1 line why) and pick **top 3–5 to A/B test** (title | category | gap), including a control near any existing title.

## Thumbnail rule-in-play (state one per report)
Pick the governing principle and say why it applies:
- **Tease, don't reveal** (default) — hint the payoff, never hand it over.
- **One focal point** — a single clear subject, no clutter.
- **Emotion on the face** — a readable human expression.
- **Contrast / transformation** — before↔after, this↔that.
- **Big readable text ≤4 words** — legible at phone size.
- **Show the result** — lead with the outcome when the outcome is the draw.

## Search vs browse
- **Browse** (default): problem-aware, highest ceiling, most views
- **Search**: solution-aware, easier early wins, specific/technical problems → use vidIQ / TubeBuddy for high-volume, low-competition keywords

## Thumbnail
1. [ ] List visual/text elements for each title component
2. [ ] Pick format: result · before/after · novelty · comparison/tier
3. [ ] Tease, don't reveal — viewer can't solve it from the thumbnail
4. [ ] Reinforces (not repeats) the title's curiosity gap

## Story & retention
- [ ] Promise stated up front
- [ ] Progress = escalating mini-payoffs / story beats
- [ ] Payoff chosen (emotional vs informational) — "did they or didn't they?"
- [ ] Start at the crash (cut the setup)
- [ ] Protagonist = the subject, not you
- [ ] Format ingredients defined: Constant / Variable / Tension / Payoff
- [ ] "Land the plane" line written (why I told you this)
