# The Framework — full method

Source: The Futur workshop, Chris Do × Aprilynne Alter. The idea sets the ceiling; execution determines the result. Everything below happens before recording.

## Stage 1 — Ideation
Idea = Topic × Format × Angle. Filter on TAM (outliers vs view goal) and Remarkability.

## Stage 2 — Title
Packaging is not the idea. Generate 30+ titles. One curiosity gap. Skimmable, upper-left weight, ~5th-grade reading level.

## Stage 3 — Click triggers
Desire, Fear, or Curiosity (Jake Thomas).

## Stage 4 — Thumbnail
List content elements. Pick result / before-after / novelty / comparison. Tease, never reveal.

## Stage 5 — Story
Promise → Progress → Payoff. Subject is the protagonist. Constant / Variable / Tension / Payoff. Land the plane.
